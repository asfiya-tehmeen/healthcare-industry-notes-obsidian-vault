## The one-paragraph version of the industry
A company finds a molecule that might treat a disease, proves in humans (clinical trials) that it is safe and works, convinces regulators (FDA/EMA) to approve it, manufactures it under strict quality rules, sells it through wholesalers/pharmacies/hospitals paid for by insurers, and keeps watching its safety forever. **Every step produces data, and every step is regulated** — that is why data + compliance people are in demand.

## Why data & compliance are the hot overlap
- Regulators can reject a drug if data is unreliable (**data integrity**).
- Systems that touch regulated data must be **validated** (proved to work as intended).
- Patient data is legally protected (**HIPAA, GDPR**).
- Standards (**CDISC, FHIR**) mean pipelines must map messy data into strict formats.
