# Drug Lifecycle Overview

## Simple explanation
Think of it as a funnel: ~10,000 compounds screened → ~250 enter preclinical → ~5 enter human trials → **1** gets approved.

## Stages & typical duration
| Stage | Years | Question answered | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Target ID & discovery | 2–4 | What do we hit and with what molecule? | Lead candidate |
| Preclinical | 1–2 | Is it safe enough to try in people? | Tox, PK data; IND filing |
| Phase I | ~1 | Safe? What dose? | Safety/PK in 20–100 people |
| Phase II | 1–2 | Does it seem to work? | Efficacy signal, 100–500 people |
| Phase III | 2–4 | Does it truly work vs. standard care? | Pivotal data, 1,000–5,000+ |
| Submission/review | 0.5–1.5 | Should it be approved? | NDA/BLA/MAA, label |
| Phase IV / post-market | ongoing | Long-term safety, real use | PV, RWE, registries |

## Key vocabulary
- **Small molecule** (pill, chemical) vs **biologic** (large, from cells) vs **gene/cell therapy**.
- **PK** (what body does to drug) vs **PD** (what drug does to body).
- **Orphan drug** – rare disease, incentives.
- **Breakthrough / Fast Track / Priority Review / Accelerated Approval** – FDA speed-ups.

## Data produced
Assay results, omics, animal studies (GLP data), clinical data (GCP), CMC (manufacturing) data, safety (PV), and real-world data. Each has different rules → see [[03-Regulatory-Compliance/GxP-Overview]].
