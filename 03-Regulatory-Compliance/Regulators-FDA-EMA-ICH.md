# Regulators: FDA, EMA, ICH & Friends

## Simple explanation
Regulators write the rules and check you follow them. Guidance documents explain *how* to comply; the actual law is in regulations (US: Code of Federal Regulations, "CFR").

## FDA (US)
Centers: **CDER** (drugs), **CBER** (biologics/vaccines), **CDRH** (devices). Offices of inspections (ORA/OII).
Key laws/regs:
- **21 CFR Part 11** – electronic records/signatures → [[21-CFR-Part-11]]
- **21 CFR Part 210/211** – cGMP for drugs
- **21 CFR Part 312** – INDs; **Part 314** – NDAs; **Part 50/56** – consent/IRB
- **21 CFR Part 820** – device quality (now QMSR aligning with ISO 13485)
- **FD&C Act**, **DSCSA** (drug supply chain security: serialization/traceability)
- FDA guidances: https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents
- eCFR (live law text): https://www.ecfr.gov

## EMA / EU
- **EudraLex** – EU rules (Vol. 4 = GMP, **Annex 11** = computerised systems, **Annex 15** = qualification/validation).
- **EU CTR (Clinical Trials Regulation 536/2014)**, CTIS portal.
- **EU MDR / IVDR** for devices.
- **EU AI Act** – high-risk AI incl. medical.
- https://www.ema.europa.eu

## ICH (global harmonization)
- **E-series (Efficacy)**: E6(R3) GCP, E2A–E2F safety reporting, E3 CSR, E9 stats, E8 trial design.
- **Q-series (Quality)**: Q7 API GMP, Q8 development, Q9 risk mgmt, Q10 quality system, Q12 lifecycle.
- **S (Safety)**, **M (Multidisciplinary)**: M4 CTD, M1 MedDRA.
- https://www.ich.org/page/ich-guidelines

## Others
- **PIC/S** – inspectorate cooperation. **WHO** – prequalification, GMP.
- **MHRA** (UK) – GxP Data Integrity guidance is excellent reading.
- **PMDA** (Japan), **NMPA** (China), **CDSCO** (India), **TGA** (Australia), **Health Canada**.

## Types of documents (know the difference)
Law/Regulation (binding) > Guidance (expectation) > Industry standard (GAMP, ISO) > Company SOP.

## Key data-integrity guidances (must read)
- FDA: *Data Integrity and Compliance with Drug CGMP* (Q&A, 2018)
- MHRA: *GxP Data Integrity Guidance and Definitions* (2018)
- WHO TRS 1033 Annex 4; PIC/S PI 041-1
- OECD GLP Data Integrity advisory
