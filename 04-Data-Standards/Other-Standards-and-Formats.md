# Other Standards & Formats You'll Meet

## Claims / admin (US)
- **X12 837** (claim), **835** (payment/remittance), **834** (enrollment), **270/271** eligibility, **278** prior auth.
- **NCPDP** – pharmacy claims & e-prescribing (SCRIPT).
- **CMS Blue Button / CCLF / MedPAR / Part D event files**.
- **T-MSIS** Medicaid data. **All-Payer Claims DBs.**

## Regulatory submissions
- **eCTD** (folders + XML backbone), **SPL** (Structured Product Labeling, DailyMed), **IDMP** (ISO identification of medicinal products; EU), **xEVMPD**.
- **E2B(R3)** – XML format to send individual safety case reports (ICSR) to regulators.
- **Define-XML**, **Dataset-JSON** (replacing SAS XPT), **ODM-XML** (clinical data exchange).

## Imaging / devices / genomics
- **DICOM** (images), **PACS**.
- **IEEE 11073**, **HL7 IHE profiles**.
- Genomics: FASTQ → BAM/CRAM → **VCF**; GA4GH standards (htsget, Beacon, Phenopackets).

## Supply chain
- **GS1** barcodes / **GTIN, SSCC**, **EPCIS** event standard for DSCSA serialization.
- **GDP** temperature mapping data.

## Quality & manufacturing
- **ISA-95** (MES/ERP), **OPC-UA** (equipment), **historian data** (OSIsoft PI), **batch records (eBR)**.

## Data-sharing principles
FAIR, OMOP, ISO 11179 metadata, **Common Data Elements**.

## Study tip
You don't memorize all; know *what each is for* and where to look it up. Interviewers like: "I'd check the E2B(R3) spec for field mapping."
