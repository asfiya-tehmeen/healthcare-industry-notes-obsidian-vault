# Pharma Commercial 101: Pricing, Access, Channels

## Simple explanation
Approval ≠ patients get the drug. Someone has to pay for it and a doctor has to prescribe it.

## Pricing & reimbursement
- **List price (WAC)** vs **net price**. Discounts via rebates to PBMs/payers for formulary placement.
- **Formulary tiers**: generic (tier 1) → preferred brand → non-preferred → specialty. **Prior authorization**, **step therapy**, **quality limits** manage use.
- **HTA bodies** (NICE UK, G-BA/IQWiG Germany, HAS France, CADTH Canada, ICER US) judge value: **QALY**, **ICER** ratio.
- **Medicare Part D** and the **Inflation Reduction Act** (price negotiation, Part D redesign, inflation rebates) – major recent change.
- **340B**, **Medicaid Best Price**, **AMP** rules.
- **International reference pricing**; India: **NPPA** price control (DPCO).

## Channels
Manufacturer → wholesaler (McKesson, Cencora, Cardinal) → retail/mail/specialty pharmacy → patient. Specialty drugs often via **hub services**, limited-distribution networks, buy-and-bill (physician buys & bills payer).

## Market access team
Payer account managers, HEOR, contracting, pricing analysts, trade/distribution, patient support (copay cards, foundations).

## Lifecycle management
Label expansions, new formulations, pediatric exclusivity, **patent cliff**, **authorized generics**, **biosimilar** interchangeability, **Orange Book / Purple Book**.

## Data/compliance links
Rebate contract calculation validation, SOX controls, government price reporting accuracy (False Claims Act exposure), anti-kickback, transparent HCP spend (Sunshine).
