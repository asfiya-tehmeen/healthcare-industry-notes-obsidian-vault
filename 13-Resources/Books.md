# Books (pick 3–4, don't read all)

## Industry understanding
- *An American Sickness* – Elisabeth Rosenthal (US system, money)
- *Bad Pharma* – Ben Goldacre (trial transparency/bias)
- *The Emperor of All Maladies* – S. Mukherjee (cancer history, readable)

## Clinical research & stats
- *Fundamentals of Clinical Trials* – Friedman, Furberg, DeMets
- *Medical Statistics at a Glance* – Petrie & Sabin
- *Causal Inference: What If* – Hernán & Robins (free: https://miguelhernan.org/whatifbook)
- *Statistical Issues in Drug Development* – Stephen Senn

## Compliance / validation
- *GAMP 5 Guide: Compliance Approach to GxP Computerized Systems* (ISPE)
- *Validating Pharmaceutical Systems* – John Andrews
- *Good Manufacturing Practices for Pharmaceuticals* – Joseph Nally

## Data & engineering
- *Designing Data-Intensive Applications* – Kleppmann
- *Fundamentals of Data Engineering* – Reis & Housley
- *The Data Warehouse Toolkit* – Kimball
- *SQL Performance Explained* – Winand (https://use-the-index-luke.com free)
- *Python for Data Analysis* – McKinney (free: https://wesmckinney.com/book/)
- *R for Data Science* (free: https://r4ds.hadley.nz)
- *The Book of OHDSI* (free)

## Health informatics
- *FHIR: The Specification in plain language* – use hl7.org/fhir instead
