# Free / Low-cost Courses & Learning Paths

## Industry & drug development
- FDA "Drug Development Process" + CDER Small Business & Industry Assistance (CDER SBIA) learn modules: https://www.fda.gov/drugs/news-events-human-drugs/cder-small-business-and-industry-assistance-sbia-learn
- Coursera (audit free): "Drug Development" (UCSD), "Understanding Clinical Research" (Vanderbilt) – search titles.
- NIDA/NIH Good Clinical Practice training: https://www.nida.nih.gov/research/research-training/good-clinical-practice-training
- GCP certification (TransCelerate mutual recognition, free via many): https://www.transceleratebiopharmainc.com
- WHO Academy / OpenWHO: https://openwho.org
- EUPATI (patient/clinical research): https://eupati.eu/

## Compliance / GxP
- CITI Program (GCP, HIPAA, ethics; often via employer): https://about.citiprogram.org
- HHS HIPAA training materials: https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/training/index.html
- ISPE webinars: https://ispe.org
- FDA CDER Learn: https://www.fda.gov/training-and-continuing-education/cder-learn
- EMA e-learning: https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/partners-networks/eu-network-training-centre

## Data standards
- CDISC Academy: https://www.cdisc.org/education
- OHDSI/EHDEN Academy: https://academy.ehden.eu
- HL7 FHIR Fundamentals (HL7 education; some free): https://www.hl7.org/training/
- Pharmaverse tutorials: https://pharmaverse.org/e2eclinical/
- PHUSE Education: https://phuse.global

## Data & engineering
- CS50 SQL / CS50P: https://cs50.harvard.edu
- Data Engineering Zoomcamp: https://github.com/DataTalksClub/data-engineering-zoomcamp
- MLOps Zoomcamp: https://github.com/DataTalksClub/mlops-zoomcamp
- Google Cloud Skills Boost (healthcare): https://www.cloudskillsboost.google
- Microsoft Learn (Azure Health Data Services): https://learn.microsoft.com
- AWS Skill Builder: https://skillbuilder.aws
- Databricks Academy free: https://www.databricks.com/learn/training
- Snowflake University: https://learn.snowflake.com
- fast.ai, Kaggle Learn: https://www.kaggle.com/learn
- R for clinical: "R for Clinical Study Reports and Submission" (free book) https://r4csr.org

## Health informatics

- AMIA 10x10: https://amia.org/education-events
- MIT Critical Data / MIMIC tutorials: https://mimic.mit.edu
- Stanford: "Medicine & AI" free materials
