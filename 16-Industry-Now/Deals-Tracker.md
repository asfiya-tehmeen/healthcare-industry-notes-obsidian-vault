# Deals to Know (M&A, Licensing, Tech Partnerships)

> **Reliability note**: 2026 items come from web searches on 2026-10-28, mostly from aggregator/trade sites that disagreed with each other on rankings and dates. Older (≤2024) deals are well documented. Before an interview, confirm any deal you plan to cite on the company's press release or Fierce/Endpoints/Reuters. Licensing "up to $X" values include milestones that may never be paid.

## How to talk about a deal (framework)
1. What was bought/licensed (asset, modality, disease)? 2. Why (patent cliff, pipeline gap, new platform)? 3. Price and structure (upfront vs milestones, CVR)? 4. Risks (trial failure, integration, antitrust)? 5. **Data/compliance implication** (system consolidation, migration validation, data governance, harmonizing QMS, privacy of acquired data). Point 5 is your edge.

## Veeva vs IQVIA (know this one)
Veeva (cloud CRM/Vault; moved off Salesforce) and IQVIA (data + services) have had litigation over data access/competition since ~2017. Why it matters: shows the commercial data/CRM ecosystem is contested; you'll hear it in interviews at either.

## Watch-list to track yourself
- BIOSECURE-type restrictions affecting Chinese CDMOs/data.
- FTC scrutiny of PBM and pharma mergers.
- Biosimilar and generic consolidation (Sandoz, Teva, Viatris, Sun, Dr. Reddy's, Cipla).
- Consulting/CRO consolidations (IQVIA, ICON, Fortrea, Parexel, Syneos).
