# Companies to Target (Data + Compliance Focus)

Tiered by how likely they are to hire for data/compliance and how realistic entry is. Verify openings on careers pages; hiring changes quarterly.

## Tier 1: Big pharma (best brand, strong GxP data teams, often have India GCCs)
Pfizer · Novartis · Roche/Genentech · Merck (MSD) · AstraZeneca · Sanofi · GSK · J&J · AbbVie · Lilly · Novo Nordisk · Bayer · Takeda · Amgen · Gilead · BMS · Boehringer Ingelheim · Novartis/AZ/Sanofi/Pfizer/GSK/Lilly/Roche global hubs in **Hyderabad, Bangalore, Mumbai, Gurgaon, Pune**.
Why: large clinical data, RWE, PV and quality orgs; invest in AI + validation.

## Tier 2: CROs / clinical service providers (easiest entry, fastest learning)
IQVIA · Parexel · ICON · Syneos · Fortrea · Labcorp Drug Development · Medpace · PPD (Thermo Fisher) · Clario (Thermo Fisher) · Cognizant/Tata/Accenture/Infosys life-sciences units · Indegene · Tech Mahindra LS.
Roles: clinical data management, SDTM/ADaM programming, PV case processing, CSV, data engineering.

## Tier 3: Health/pharma tech & data companies (best for data engineering)
Veeva · Medidata (Dassault) · Oracle Health · Epic · Flatiron · Tempus · Komodo · Truveta · Datavant · Optum · Palantir (Foundry in pharma) · Snowflake/Databricks life-sciences teams · Certara · Simulations Plus · Benchling · Dotmatics · Schrödinger · Recursion · Insilico.

## Tier 4: Consulting & analytics boutiques (fast learning, interview-heavy)
ZS Associates · IQVIA Consulting · Axtria · Tredence · Fractal · Mu Sigma-type · Deloitte/EY/PwC/KPMG Life Sciences (compliance, validation, GxP) · Accenture Life Sciences · Cognizant · Genpact · EXL.

## Tier 5: Indian pharma & generics (if you want India-based core pharma)
Sun Pharma (also acquiring Organon) · Dr. Reddy's · Cipla · Lupin · Zydus · Aurobindo · Biocon · Glenmark · Torrent · Alkem · Divi's · Syngene · Piramal · Serum Institute · Bharat Biotech.
Roles: QA/QC data integrity, regulatory affairs, CSV, SAP/MES, analytics. Many have FDA-inspected plants → **data integrity expertise is prized**.

## Tier 6: Providers/payers (for healthcare, not pharma)
UnitedHealth/Optum · CVS Health/Aetna · Elevance · Cigna/Evernorth · Humana · Kaiser · HCA · Mayo · Cleveland Clinic; India: Apollo, Fortis, Manipal, Max.

## Tier 7: Medtech
Medtronic · Abbott · Siemens Healthineers · GE HealthCare · Philips · Boston Scientific · Stryker · BD · Baxter. Roles: QMS/ISO 13485, software validation (IEC 62304), complaint data analytics.

## How to choose
| Your priority | Aim for |
|---|---|
| Learn fastest, easiest entry | CRO / services (Tier 2, 4) |
| Best data engineering craft | Tier 3 tech, big pharma data platforms |
| Compliance depth | Pharma QA/CSV, consulting GxP practices, Indian pharma with FDA exposure |
| Long-term stability + brand | Tier 1 via GCC |

## Application playbook
1. Build a list of 40 companies; track in a sheet (company, role, contact, status, date).
2. For each, read latest press release, pipeline page, 10-K/annual report summary, and 1 recent news item (use in cover note).
3. Search roles with keywords: *Clinical Data Engineer, SDTM, GxP, CSV, Data Integrity, Veeva, RIM, PV Analyst, RWE, OMOP, Data Governance*.
4. Reach out to 3 people per company on LinkedIn with a specific, short message (don't ask for a job; ask for a 15-min insight call).
5. Apply through referrals where possible.
