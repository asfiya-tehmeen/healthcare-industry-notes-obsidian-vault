# Industry Trends You Must Know (as of Oct 2026)

> Compiled from web searches on 2026-10-28. Sources are mostly secondary (consultancies, trade press). Treat figures as approximate and re-check before quoting in an interview. Source list at bottom.

## 1. Patent cliff → dealmaking
Between ~2025 and 2030, drugs worth **$300B+ in global revenue** lose exclusivity (oncology, immunology, metabolic). Big pharma has cash but thin pipelines, so it buys biotechs. Result: 2026 M&A is running well above 2025. See [[Deals-Tracker]].
*Why you care*: more M&A = system/data migrations, integration, validation and data-governance work.

## 2. GLP-1 / obesity boom
Lilly (Mounjaro/Zepbound) and Novo (Ozempic/Wegovy) dominate. 2026 themes: **oral versions** (Novo's Wegovy pill approved Dec 2025), more entrants, price pressure, employer/payer coverage fights, compounding.
*Data angle*: huge RWE questions (adherence/persistence, outcomes beyond weight loss), supply-chain forecasting, claims analytics. Great interview talking point.

## 3. US drug pricing politics
- **Most-Favored-Nation (MFN)** pricing pressure and **TrumpRx** direct-to-consumer platform (launched Feb 2026).
- **Inflation Reduction Act** Medicare price negotiation continues.
- Several companies struck deals with the administration (pricing concessions in exchange for tariff relief).
- **PBM reform** debates.
*Data angle*: gross-to-net modelling, pricing analytics, rebate/contract compliance.

## 4. Tariffs & onshoring
Tariff threats on imported pharma pushed companies to announce large **US manufacturing investments**. Reported actual tariff impact so far is more limited than feared. Supply-chain resilience (India/China API dependence) remains a theme.
*Data angle*: supply chain visibility, serialization, manufacturing analytics.

## 5. AI everywhere – and regulators catching up
- Big pharma–tech alliances (Lilly–NVIDIA, Merck–Google Cloud, Novo–OpenAI, etc.). See [[Deals-Tracker]].
- **FDA–EMA joint principles for AI in drug development** (Jan 2026, 10 non-binding principles).
- **FDA draft guidance** on AI to support regulatory decision-making (Jan 2025; finalization reportedly pending).
- **EU AI Act** obligations phasing in (high-risk rules around Aug 2026; verify exact dates).
- **EU GMP Annex 22** (AI in manufacturing) under consultation – favors static/deterministic models, human oversight.
- Industry guide: ISPE GAMP AI guidance.
*Data/compliance angle (YOUR niche)*: validating AI = credibility assessment, context of use, data lineage, monitoring drift, human-in-the-loop, audit trails for prompts/outputs. This is the hottest crossover of data + compliance.

## 6. Computer Software Assurance (CSA) & modern validation
Shift from document-heavy CSV to risk-based, test-smart validation; "validation as code" in CI/CD. (I could not confirm in my search whether FDA's CSA guidance is now final — check fda.gov.)

## 7. Clinical trial modernization
ICH **E6(R3)** GCP (principles + Annex 1 finalized 2025) pushes risk-proportionate, quality-by-design and fit-for-purpose technology/data. Also: decentralized & hybrid trials, EU CTR/CTIS, risk-based monitoring, eConsent, digital endpoints, synthetic/external control arms, **Dataset-JSON** replacing SAS XPT.

## 8. Real-world data & interoperability
FHIR mandates, TEFCA, Bulk FHIR, OMOP adoption (EHDEN, DARWIN EU), privacy-preserving tokenization, federated analysis, and EHDS (European Health Data Space).

## 9. New modalities
Cell & gene therapy, radiopharmaceuticals, ADCs (antibody-drug conjugates), bispecifics, mRNA, in-vivo CAR-T, RNAi. Different manufacturing/supply/cold-chain/data needs (vein-to-vein tracking for autologous therapies).

## 10. China as innovation source
Western pharma is **in-licensing from Chinese biotechs** at record levels (AstraZeneca–CSPC, BMS–Hengrui, Roche–Hansoh, etc.). Also rising geopolitical/BIOSECURE-type scrutiny of Chinese CDMOs and data.

## 11. Cost, productivity, layoffs, restructuring
R&D returns recovering mostly due to a few mega-blockbusters. Companies cut costs, consolidate sites, and centralize in **GCCs (Global Capability Centers)**, especially India → relevant for your job hunt.

## 12. Cybersecurity & data protection
Healthcare breaches (e.g. Change Healthcare 2024) drive stricter security and vendor-risk requirements. HIPAA Security Rule update proposed. Ransomware is a GxP availability risk.

## 13. Biosimilars & generics shifts
Stelara/Humira-type biosimilar erosion, interchangeability, generic shortages, India's generics players expanding into complex generics/biosimilars.
