# More Relevant Topics (short primers)

Each is a quick, plain-English primer so nothing is a blind spot. Search the keyword to go deeper later.

## Science & clinical
- **Biologics vs small molecules** – small = chemical pills, easy to copy (generics). Biologics = made in living cells, complex, copies are "biosimilars".
- **Precision / personalized medicine & biomarkers** – treat based on genes/markers; **companion diagnostics** (test needed to get the drug).
- **Cell & gene therapy** – modify a patient's cells/genes (CAR-T, CRISPR). Manufacturing is per-patient ("vein-to-vein") → chain-of-identity tracking.
- **Vaccines** – prophylactic; mRNA platforms; cold chain; huge trial sizes.
- **Rare/orphan diseases** – small populations, special incentives.
- **Pharmacology basics** – PK/PD, half-life, bioavailability, bioequivalence, dose-response.
- **Medical devices & SaMD** – software as medical device; FDA classes I–III; 510(k)/PMA.
- **Companion topics**: radiopharma, ADCs, peptides, microbiome.

## Regulatory & legal
- **IP**: patents, data exclusivity, **Orange/Purple Book**, patent thickets, evergreening, **Paragraph IV** challenges.
- **Hatch-Waxman** (generics path), **BPCIA** (biosimilars).
- **Expedited programs**: Fast Track, Breakthrough, Priority Review, Accelerated Approval, RMAT.
- **REMS / RMP** risk programs.
- **Pediatric rules** (PREA/BPCA, EU PIP).
- **Anti-kickback, False Claims Act, Sunshine/Open Payments, FCPA** – compliance heavy in commercial.
- **Labeling & promotion**: on-label vs off-label; MLR review.
- **Clinical trial transparency**: registration, results posting, EU CTR/CTIS, data sharing, **TransCelerate**.
- **ICH E6(R3), E8(R1) (quality by design), E9(R1) estimands, M11 (structured protocol – CeSHarP)**.
- **IDMP** (identification of medicinal products), **SPOR** (EU master data).
- **ISO standards**: ISO 13485 (devices QMS), ISO 27001 (security), ISO 14971 (device risk), ISO 11179 (metadata).
- **SOX / financial controls** (for public company IT general controls: access, change mgmt, backups).

## Data & technology
- **Master Data Management (MDM)** – single golden record for HCP, HCO, product, patient (Reltio, Veeva Network, Informatica).
- **Data governance, catalog, lineage, stewardship, data products/data mesh**.
- **FAIR data**, metadata standards, ontologies, knowledge graphs.
- **Dimensional modeling, SCD2, Data Vault** for pharma warehouses.
- **Statistical Computing Environments (SCE)** – validated platforms where SAS/R/Python run for submissions (Posit, Domino, SAS Viya, Databricks).
- **R/Python in submissions** – R Consortium's *R Submissions Working Group* pilots; open-source validation (**{riskmetric}, pharmaR**).
- **Electronic Lab Notebook / LIMS / MES / QMS / eTMF / RIM / CTMS / IRT** – know the system acronyms.
- **IDMP & eCTD 4.0**; **eTMF reference model**; **CDISC 360i**, **USDM** (digital protocol model).
- **Digital twins, synthetic control arms, in-silico trials**.
- **Federated learning & privacy-preserving analytics**; **TEEs**; **differential privacy**.
- **Generative AI**: RAG on SOPs/regulatory docs, medical writing drafts, CSR automation, coding/auto-narratives. Needs validation + human oversight.
- **MLOps & model risk management**: drift, bias, explainability, model registries, audit logging.
- **Cybersecurity**: zero trust, SOC 2, HITRUST, incident response, ransomware recovery for GxP.
- **Cloud validation**: shared-responsibility model, SaaS vendor qualification, infrastructure-as-code qualification.
- **Blockchain in supply chain** (niche), **IoT/cold chain sensors**.

## Business & commercial
- **Payer landscape**: PBMs, formularies, rebates, 340B, Medicare Part D redesign, IRA negotiation.
- **Value-based contracts / outcomes-based agreements**.
- **HEOR/ health economics**: QALY, cost-effectiveness, budget impact, HTA.
- **Launch excellence**, patient support/hub services, specialty pharmacy, DTC platforms (TrumpRx, Lilly Direct, Pfizer Direct).
- **Omnichannel marketing, field force analytics, next-best-action**.
- **Biotech funding**: VC, IPO, royalty financing, CVRs; why biotechs get acquired.
- **R&D productivity**: probability of success, NPV, portfolio decisions.
- **Contract manufacturing (CDMO) economics**, outsourcing trends.

## Global/India specific
- **CDSCO**, **NPPA price control**, **Schedule M GMP revision**, **PLI scheme for pharma**, **USFDA inspections of Indian plants**, **Jan Aushadhi / generics**, **DPDP Act 2023**, **ABDM (Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission) health IDs & FHIR-based stack**, **Indian GCC ecosystem**, **India clinical trial rules (New Drugs and Clinical Trials Rules 2019)**.
- Search: "CDSCO", "ABDM", "Schedule M revised".

## Soft/professional skills that matter in regulated industries
Documentation discipline, writing clear SOPs/requirements, risk-based thinking, stakeholder management with QA/clinical/IT, saying "show me the evidence", calm during audits.
